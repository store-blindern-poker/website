How the numbers move

Points in one minute

Show up: +5,000
Every night starts with an attendance bonus, credited before anything else. Consistency is the most reliable way up this leaderboard.
Play the night
Your buy-in comes off your points; your final chip stack goes back on. Finish above what you paid in and the night was a win.
Champions are forever
The leaderboard resets to 40,000 each semester, but season champions keep their title for good: the hall of fame is permanent.

The full points system, including the one-top-up rule and a worked example, lives on the rules page.
