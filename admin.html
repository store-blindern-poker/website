Season

Seasons are frozen and started here, once a semester. The current season is named in the line under the page title.

This freezes the current season for good: its leaderboard becomes a permanent record and no more nights can be settled into it. Everyone starts the new season on the starting points below. Do this once per semester, never mid-season.

Members

…

Real names and emails appear here and nowhere else on the site. Everywhere public, a member is their pseudonym.

Members now give a first name and a last name on a step of their own, and those are shown under the full name. A row badged looks like their pseudonym has a pseudonym stored where the real name should be, from before that step existed. Worth asking them at the next night.

Removing a member hides them from this list, the leaderboard and the pickers. Nothing is deleted: their nights stay in the database and Restore brings all of it back. The one catch is the pseudonym, which is released when they go, so somebody else can claim it.
