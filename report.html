2 · The top-up

One top-up per night, points for chips 1:1, and it can only bring you back to tonight's stack. Most people take it at the first break, around 19:15, when everybody is at the bank anyway. It stays open for as long as the night is open, so there is nothing to miss.

2 · No top-up tonight

Your season points are all in play already, so there is no top-up allowance left for tonight. Nothing is wrong: play the stack you have. An organiser can talk it through if this looks off.

2 · The bank has packed up

The round is over, so the bank has stopped issuing top-ups. Reporting is still open: count your chips and close the round below.

3 · Close the round

When you are done for the night, count your chips and report the total. You get a closing slip to show an organiser while you hand the chips back. No rush: reporting stays open until the morning after.
