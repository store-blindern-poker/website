2 · The re-buy

One re-buy per night, points for chips 1:1, and it can only bring you back to tonight's stack. Most people take it at the first break, around 19:15, when everybody is at the bank anyway. The bank is open now. Tap below and show the slip when you collect: chips only leave the bank against a slip.

2 · The bank has shut

Reporting has closed for tonight, so there is no re-buy to take now. An organiser can still sort out anything that is wrong.

2 · The night is settled

The night has been settled, so the bank has shut and there is no re-buy to take. If something in your numbers is wrong, tell an organiser: a settled night can be corrected and settled again.

2 · No re-buy tonight

Your season points are all in play already, so there is no re-buy allowance left for tonight. Nothing is wrong: play the stack you have. An organiser can talk it through if this looks off.

2 · The bank has packed up

The round is over, so the bank has stopped issuing re-buys. Reporting is still open: count your chips and close the round below.

3 · Close the round

When you are done for the night, count your chips and report the total. You get a closing slip to show an organiser while you hand the chips back. No rush: reporting stays open until the organisers settle the night. If you go home without reporting, an organiser can still enter your numbers by hand.
